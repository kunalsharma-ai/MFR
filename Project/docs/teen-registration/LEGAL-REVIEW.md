# Teen registration: points for legal review

No legal text was written for this work. The consent and terms text is the client's own (MINOR-2026.10 and the Teen
assent statements), shown word for word. These points need a decision before launch:

1. **Legal review of the consent texts** (guardian consent MINOR-2026.10, teen assent, Teen research terms).
2. **Section 14 of the consent mentions payouts.** The Teen Panel gives reward points only, no cash. The text should
   match.
3. **No dedicated Teen privacy notice.** The pages link to the general Privacy Policy and the "Teen privacy information"
   section of the terms. Many regimes expect a notice written for children / teens.
4. **Verifiable parental consent.** Today the guardian is verified by control of the email address. Some countries
   (e.g. the US under COPPA for under-13s, which this flow excludes, and several GDPR member states with ages 13–16)
   expect stronger or different methods. Country-specific rules may be needed.
5. **Age of digital consent varies** (13–16 across the EU). The flow asks for guardian consent for everyone aged 13–17,
   which is at least as strict, but the wording of notices may need to vary by country.
6. **DOB vs. "no exact DOB"**: the questionnaire notes that an exact date of birth shouldn't be collected, while the
   registration needs it for age checks. A decision is needed on what is stored after the check.
7. **Retention**: declined, expired and cancelled registrations keep the decision record. A retention period should be set.
